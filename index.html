Flexible Booking Engines

Choose the easiest way for your customers to book your services online. No technical skills required.

Approach A: The "Iframe Widget"

The booking engine can be hosted as a standalone portal or injected into any existing website (Wix, WordPress, Squarespace) via a secure, drop-in Iframe.

  • Data Sync: Automatically retrieves precise property data (like square footage) as customers type their address.
  • Instant Quoting: Calculates exact cost and estimated duration in real-time.

Approach B: The "Hosted Portal"

A client-facing engine designed to maximize conversion rates through a frictionless, transparent checkout process.

  • Dynamic Discounts: Intelligently applies and displays multi-service discounts dynamically.
  • Stripe Checkout: Highly secure, PCI-compliant payment gateway built directly into the flow.

Core Operational Features

Everything you need to manage operations, customize rules, and track business growth.

Admin Command Center

Key Metrics: Real-time data including active jobs, MTD revenue, and customer satisfaction scores.

Visual Analytics: Easy-to-read charts tracking weekly revenue fluctuations and service distribution.

Intuitive Navigation: Quick access to Jobs, Leads & Traffic, Team Members, Scheduling, and Settings.

Job Operations Control

Centralized Tracking: Monitor active cleaning contracts, track processing actions, and handle transaction balances.

Comprehensive Details: Displays unique invoice numbers, customer contact info, and operational state.

Actionable Management: Direct control over status to collect payments, put/release holds, or complete scopes.

Service & Rate Factors

Dynamic Pricing: Total control over the mathematical formulas used to price your jobs.

Granular Config: Configure base rates, square-footage multipliers, fixed-fee charges, and time estimates.

Streamlined Actions: Quickly add new services, toggle visibility, and set customer-facing display order.

Dispatch & Schedule

Automated Slots: Automatically reserves the exact calendar slots required to complete a secured job.

Visual Calendar: A comprehensive weekly view displaying color-coded job blocks and daily hours.

Drag-and-Drop: Easily reorganize the fleet's workload to reschedule assignments or dispatch units.

System Setup & Branding

Custom Branding: Maintain unique identity by managing customer-facing UI, logos, and color palettes.

Core Definition: Define operational scope by selecting specific industry types (e.g., "Softwash").

Operational Toggles: Granular control over features like toggling the automated "Property Data Lookup".

Fleet & Crew Dispatch

Vehicle Assignments: Manage vehicle assignments and designate specific crews to your fleet.

Status Tracking: Monitor real-time availability to instantly see which vehicles are scheduled or idle.

Shift Windows: Set precise working hours and shift dates to keep your daily operations organized.
